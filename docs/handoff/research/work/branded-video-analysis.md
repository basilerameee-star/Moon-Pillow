## Core model

A niche Shopify brand, preferably with custom or logo-marked products. Storebuild.ai/Zendrop build the initial storefront; ImportYeti helps identify established brands' manufacturers; Claude connected to Zendrop helps shortlist products and marketing angles; AdLibrary.io supplies creative references; ChatGPT generates static ad imagery and Canva adds text; Facebook/Instagram ads drive traffic; Zendrop processes fulfillment.

This is a branded store and Meta advertising method, distinct from the Romas Google Performance Max large-catalog workflow. The actual ad campaign setup and testing rules are deferred to other videos.
